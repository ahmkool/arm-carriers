## What you're looking for (before you click)

| Signal | Good fit | Skip |
|--------|----------|------|
| **Viewer count** | ~15–500 live (sweet spot for indie outreach) | 0–5 (too small) or 2,000+ (hard to reach) |
| **Format** | 2 players on mic, couch/local co-op, "playing with friend/wife/brother" | Solo story mode, speedrun solo only |
| **Genre vibe** | Chaotic co-op, party indies, "we're bad at this" energy | Competitive ranked, MMO, single-player only |
| **Recency** | Streamed anchor game in last 30–90 days | Never played anything like your game |

## Full walkthrough — PlateUp! (repeat for every anchor game)

### Step 1 — Open the game directory

1. Click your **Twitch address bar**.
2. Paste: **https://www.twitch.tv/directory/game/PlateUp%21**
3. Press **Enter**.

You should land on the **PlateUp!** category page with a header showing the game name and follower count.

> **If the page looks empty or says "No live channels":** that's normal for niche games. Continue to Step 4 (Videos) and Step 5 (Search). The value is often in **past broadcasts**, not live right now.

---

### Step 2 — Find **live** channels playing PlateUp!

On the PlateUp! directory page:

1. Look for tabs under the game title: **Live Channels** | **Videos** | **Clips** (exact labels may vary slightly).
2. Click **Live Channels** if it isn't already selected.
3. Scan the channel list. Each row shows:
   - **Streamer name** (clickable)
   - **Stream title**
   - **Viewer count** (number on the right, e.g. `47`, `128`)

**Filter mentally to 15–500 viewers:**

- Click a channel only if the viewer count is in your range.
- Open promising ones in a **new tab** (right-click → **Open link in new tab**) so you don't lose the directory list.

**On a live stream — check co-op in ~30 seconds:**

1. **Title/tags:** Look for "co-op", "couch", "with [name]", "2 player", "local".
2. **Video layout:** Two face cams, split screen, or one cam with a second person on the couch.
3. **Audio:** Two distinct voices reacting together.
4. **Chat:** Ask yourself "Would this person play Armed Together with a partner?" — party/co-op indies are the signal.

If it fits → add to spreadsheet (template at the end). If not → close tab, back to the list.

---

### Step 3 — Sort and skim more live results

On **Live Channels**:

1. If there's a **Sort** control (often **Viewers: High to Low**), use it to surface mid-size streamers first.
2. Work **top to bottom** until you've checked everyone in the 15–500 band.
3. Note channels that are **offline now** but have PlateUp! in recent stream history — you'll catch them in Step 4.

---

### Step 4 — Find **past broadcasts / VODs** (often the goldmine)

Many co-op indie streamers aren't live on PlateUp! when you search — VODs still identify them.

1. On the same PlateUp! page, click the **Videos** tab.
2. You'll see **Past Broadcasts** (and sometimes Highlights).
3. Each entry shows:
   - Channel name
   - Stream title
   - **Views** and **time since upload** (e.g. "3 days ago")

**What to do:**

1. Click a VOD whose title mentions co-op, duo, couch, or a partner's name.
2. On the VOD page, check the **channel name** (top left) — click it to open their channel.
3. On their channel, open the **Videos** tab → confirm they play **other** co-op indies (Moving Out, Overcooked, etc.), not just one-off PlateUp! streams.

**Viewer count from VODs (estimate "avg viewers"):**

- Live count is exact; for VOD-only channels, use:
  - **Recent live streams** on their channel (if any replays show "X viewers" in title/thumbnail era), or
  - Open **https://www.twitch.tv/sullygnome** (third-party) and search the channel for average viewers — optional, not required for this method.
- Rule of thumb: **VOD views** on a 2–4 hour co-op stream — if a PlateUp! VOD has hundreds–low thousands of views and they stream weekly, they're often in your 15–500 band when live.

Add strong VOD-only leads to the spreadsheet with a note: `Found via PlateUp! VOD — verify live CC`.

---

### Step 5 — Twitch **Search** (backup when directory is thin)

1. Paste: **https://www.twitch.tv/search?term=PlateUp%21**
2. Press **Enter**.
3. Use the filter pills: **Channels** | **Categories** | **Videos**.
4. Click **Videos** → skim recent PlateUp! broadcasts from channels you didn't see on the directory page.
5. Click **Channels** → see channels with PlateUp! in name or content (fewer results, still worth a look).

Repeat search with co-op keywords if needed:

- https://www.twitch.tv/search?term=PlateUp%21%20co-op
- https://www.twitch.tv/search?term=PlateUp%21%20couch%20co-op

---

### Step 6 — Open the channel page and collect outreach info

For each promising streamer, on their channel (`https://www.twitch.tv/CHANNELNAME`):

1. **About panel** (below the video, or click **About** on mobile layout):
   - **Email** (business email line)
   - **Discord** link
   - **Twitter/X**, **YouTube**, **Linktree**
2. **Schedule** (if listed) — note timezone and co-op-friendly slots.
3. **Videos** tab → sort mentally by "do they regularly play 2P co-op indies?"
4. Copy **channel URL**: `https://www.twitch.tv/USERNAME` (username is lowercase in the URL).

**Co-host check (deeper):**

- Watch 2–3 minutes of a co-op VOD: same partner across multiple streams = strong lead for Armed Together.
- Check **Clips** tab for duo moments.
- Panels sometimes say "Stream with my husband/wife/cohost [name]".

---

### Step 7 — Record in your spreadsheet

Create columns like this:

| Column | Example | Notes |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **Channel name** | `CozyCouchGamers` | Display name |
| **Twitch link** | `https://www.twitch.tv/cozycouchgamers` | Canonical URL |
| **Avg viewers** | `85` | Live number if seen; else `~60 est` from VOD/SullyGnome |
| **Anchor game** | `PlateUp!` | Which comp game you found them through |
| **Co-op?** | `Yes — regular duo w/ partner` | Y/N + who (friend/spouse/cohost) |
| **Email** | `business@...` | From About panel |
| **Discord** | `discord.gg/...` | Or "DM on Twitch only" |
| **Other co-op games** | `Moving Out, Tools Up` | Proves fit for Armed Together |
| **Last streamed anchor** | `2026-03-10` | Recency |
| **Status** | `To contact` | To contact / Emailed / No response / Interested |
| **Notes** | `Chaotic energy, 2 face cams, EU evenings` | Personalization for outreach |

**One row per channel.** If you find the same streamer via Moving Out later, add `Moving Out` to **Other co-op games** or merge rows — don't duplicate.

## Repeat for the other anchor games

Same 7 steps for each URL — only the game name changes:

1. Open directory URL (table at top).
2. **Live Channels** → 15–500 viewers → co-op check.
3. Sort/skim live list.
4. **Videos** → past broadcasts → channel → co-op history.
5. **Search** URL for that game (+ `co-op` if needed).
6. Channel **About** → email/Discord.
7. Spreadsheet row.

**Suggested order** (overlap audience with Armed Together):

1. PlateUp! (walkthrough above)
2. Moving Out
3. Tools Up
4. Unrailed!
5. Heave Ho
6. Ship of Fools
7. Biped

Budget **15–20 minutes per game** for a first pass. You're building a list, not watching full streams.
